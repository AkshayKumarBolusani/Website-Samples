Questions

Before you book.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 10am to 8pm.

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the booking form on this page. You can also call us on +91 89774 78391. We will confirm your slot and the price.

Why do some services not have a fixed price?

For hair services the price depends on hair length and volume. For waxing it depends on the wax type and body area, and for facials on the facial you choose. Message us and we will give you the exact price before you book.

How many acne treatment sittings will I need?

The treatment is ₹2,500 per sitting. The number of sittings depends on your skin concern, so we recommend sharing your concern with us first.

What are the bridal makeup prices?

Party makeup is ₹6,000, engagement makeup ₹10,000, reception makeup ₹12,000 and bridal makeup ₹15,000. Message us your date to check availability.

Do you offer training?

Yes, Stay Young is also a training institute. Message us to ask about courses.

Where are you located?
